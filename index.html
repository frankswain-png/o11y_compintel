Datadog

Active

Datadog Summit San Francisco was held on 23 September, 9:00am to 5:30pm at the Hyatt Regency San Francisco — a free, one-day event billed as giving attendees a first look at what's new across the Datadog platform, including Bits AI releases, expanded agentic capabilities, new security features and improved data reliability for AI apps. Sessions featured engineers from Anthropic, Riot Games, Figma and Cognition discussing how they scale observability end-to-end and what it takes to run AI in production.

As of this report, no dedicated recap blog post or press release had been published on datadoghq.com covering what was actually shown on stage, so the specific product names and general-availability statuses behind "Bits AI releases" and "new security features" are not yet independently confirmed. This is consistent with Datadog's pattern of announcing at the DASH flagship event in June and using regional Summits primarily for enablement and community engagement rather than net-new product launches — the São Paulo Summit on 15 September similarly produced no distinct product news beyond DASH follow-through.

Watch signal: the event's framing — bundling agentic capabilities, security and AI-app reliability into one narrative — signals where Datadog wants the conversation to sit heading into its next earnings cycle. Once concrete capability names surface, they are likely to sharpen the comparison with Elastic's own AI and security integration story; until then, treat the Summit as a marketing signal rather than a product one.

Standing items: the most recent substantive Datadog news remains from before this window — Chief Product Officer Yanbing Li's 14 September post on Datadog being named "Company to Beat" for observability platforms in Gartner's AI Vendor Race research, and a 13 September trade recap of Olivier Pomel's 8 September Citi appearance disclosing that Bits AI pricing is moving to credits partly decoupled from ingest. Latest reported financials are Q2 FY2026 (6 August): revenue $1.12 billion, up 36% year over year, and roughly 4,720 customers with $100,000-or-more ARR, up 23%. Datadog was named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Observability Platforms on 15 July. No Q3 FY2026 earnings date has been announced. Remaining 2026 Summits are Paris (15 October), Seoul (27 October), Chicago (3 November), Munich (12 November) and Tokyo (16 December).
