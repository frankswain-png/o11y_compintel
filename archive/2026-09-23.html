ClickHouse

Active

ClickHouse appointed Michael Scarpelli, the former Chief Financial Officer of Snowflake, ServiceNow and Data Domain, to its Board of Directors on 22 September, where he will serve as an independent director and chair the Audit Committee. Scarpelli took all three of his previous companies through their IPOs, and CEO Aaron Katz framed the hire explicitly around scaling ClickHouse's enterprise motion. The announcement disclosed that ClickHouse has surpassed $350 million in annualised run-rate revenue, up from roughly $200 million at the end of 2025, and now counts more than 4,000 customers including DoorDash, Ramp, Meta, Tesla, Cisco and Visa. The company employs nearly 800 people across 27 countries, plans to reach 1,000 by year-end, and derives more than half its revenue from outside the United States.

The appointment follows the 2025 hire of CFO Jimmy Sexton, also a Snowflake finance alumnus, and reads as a deliberate build-out of governance and finance infrastructure around a company still operating as a private, venture-backed business at a $15 billion valuation. Separately in the window, a 21 September engineering post recapped a Moniepoint benchmark of Postgres on local NVMe storage — 9.2x higher throughput and a drop from 36.9ms to 4.0ms median write latency versus network-attached storage — and disclosed that WalShadow, ClickHouse's Postgres-to-ClickHouse replication engine, is now in private preview on ClickHouse Managed Postgres. A 22 September customer story covered hiring platform Fountain rebuilding its data plane on ClickHouse Cloud.

Watch signal: bringing in a three-time IPO CFO with deep Snowflake ties is a credibility signal aimed at enterprise buyers and, eventually, public markets — it raises the bar for ClickHouse's ClickStack pitch to be taken as seriously as an incumbent's roadmap rather than a fast-growing open-source project's, which is the comparison Elastic should be prepared to meet in competitive deals.

Standing items: Open House Bengaluru concluded 22 September with the Click-a-Thon hackathon final on the main stage; the $350 million run-rate figure disclosed with the Scarpelli appointment supersedes the $250 million ARR and 4,000-customer figures disclosed 27 May. The $15 billion valuation dates to the $400 million Series D of 16 January, whose investor base now includes J.P. Morgan Private Capital, BDT & MSD Partners, Craft Ventures and 20VC. On-Demand Compute remains in private preview ahead of a 24 September webinar; the London User Conference is scheduled for 30 September, and the Open House Roadshow continues in Munich on 6 October.

New Relic

Active

New Relic published its 2026 Observability Forecast on 22 September, produced with Enterprise Technology Research and based on a survey of 2,575 IT and engineering leaders, up from 1,700 respondents last year. For the second year running, AI is the single biggest force pulling organisations toward observability: autonomous AI agents are already in production at most surveyed organisations, but a full quarter of them have gone into production with no monitoring in place at all. Two-thirds of organisations say AI now generates or substantially rewrites more than half of their code each week, and 83% agree this makes reliable observability more critical than ever. The average cost of a high-impact outage eased slightly to about $74 million a year, but frequency is not falling — more than a third of organisations now experience one weekly or more, and the share hitting multiple outages a day has roughly tripled year over year. After two straight years of consolidation, the average number of observability tools per organisation is trending back up from around four to five, which the report attributes to a wave of narrow, AI-specific point tools.

The same day, New Relic shipped Compound Alerts, a feature that correlates up to five related alert conditions — using AND, OR and NOT logic, including across accounts — into a single actionable issue rather than a flood of separate notifications. New Relic positions it between Smart Alerts and Ground Truth in its Intelligent Observability Platform stack, each layer feeding richer context to the next toward what the company calls Autonomous Operations. A companion post on building token-efficient SRE agents was also published 22 September.

Watch signal: New Relic's own survey data — tool counts rising again after two years of consolidation, a quarter of production AI agents with zero monitoring — is useful ammunition for any vendor making a platform-consolidation argument, Elastic included, but it also complicates New Relic's own single-platform pitch if customers read the reversal as evidence that no single vendor has actually solved tool sprawl.

Standing items: New Relic remains private under Francisco Partners and TPG since November 2023 and publishes no current ARR or customer-count figures. New Relic Lens and New Relic Ground Truth both reached general availability on 15 September, just before this window. A Fleet Control governance change requiring a second reviewer before a configuration version can deploy took effect 22 September. The Microsoft Azure Marketplace channel, cited 2 June as showing double-digit year-over-year growth in committed bookings, remains the route by which New Relic most often appears opposite Elastic in Azure-committed accounts. o11y Hacks London at Clays Soho is scheduled for 30 September and Money20/20 in Las Vegas runs 18–21 October.
