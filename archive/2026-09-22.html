Datadog

Quiet

Nothing published in the 18–21 September window. The most recent Datadog activity remains 14 September, when Chief Product Officer Yanbing Li announced Datadog's "Company to Beat" positioning in Gartner's AI Vendor Race research for observability platforms, alongside a 13 September trade recap of Olivier Pomel's 8 September Citi Global TMT fireside covering AI credits pricing for Bits AI. Standing items: latest reported financials remain Q2 FY2026 (released 6 August) — revenue $1.12 billion, up 36% year over year, non-GAAP operating income $257 million, free cash flow $279 million, roughly 4,720 customers with ARR of $100,000 or more (up 23%), and full-year guidance of $4.45–$4.47 billion. Quiet

ClickHouse

Quiet

No significant news landed in the 18–21 September window. The only in-window item is minor: a community travel recap published 18 September covering conference appearances at PGDay Lowlands and Percona Live Amsterdam, not a product or business announcement. The preceding week was busy by comparison — the September newsletter, the official ClickHouse provider for Apache Airflow, and ClickHouse's listing on the dbt platform all shipped 16–17 September, and the 26.8 release, On-Demand Compute private preview, WalShadow, AI Functions in ClickHouse and CostBench's first end-to-end results (a reported 412–1,996× performance-per-dollar advantage over Snowflake, BigQuery and Redshift Serverless) all landed 8–11 September — all now outside this window. Standing items: RunReveal, a security analytics platform built on ClickHouse, was acquired on 1 September for undisclosed terms; commercial standing remains $250 million ARR and more than 4,000 customers as disclosed on 27 May, on a $15 billion valuation from the $400 million Series D of 16 January. The Open House Roadshow reaches Bangalore on 22 September and London on 30 September, both just ahead of this window's close.

Dynatrace

Quiet

Nothing published in the 18–21 September window. Dynatrace's press release archive shows nothing since 25 August, and the most recent open-source item, the dtwiz CLI for automated observability deployment, shipped 2 September — both now outside this window. Quiet
